Which community performs best

Newest community at the top. The # column is each community's rank for the selected period.

Overall score = 60% unique clicks per 100 members per tracked link + 25% replies & reactions per 100 members per post + 15% read rate, each compared to the best community (best = 100). Per-member and per-post rates keep big and small, old and new communities comparable: a community started this week is not punished for having had fewer posts. A community with fewer than 2 posts and 2 tracked links in the period is shown as "Too early to rank" instead of getting a score. Read rate only exists for posts sent from the linked number; a community without such posts is scored on clicks and engagement alone.
